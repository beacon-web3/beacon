# White Paper Outline

This is the working outline for a future Beacon white paper.

The white paper should explain the product, incentives, treasury, governance, and risks without using hype language or promising returns.

## 1. Abstract

Summarize Beacon as a decentralized discovery network that rewards early, high-quality curation.

## 2. The Discovery Problem

Explain why discovery becomes harder as content volume increases.

Topics:

* AI-assisted content abundance.
* Attention as the scarce resource.
* Limits of likes, ratings, reviews, search, and social feeds.

## 3. Beacon's Core Thesis

Explain conviction-based discovery.

Key claim:

> A recommendation backed by economic commitment and public reputation is a stronger signal than a passive like.

## 4. MVP Category: Books

Explain why books are the first category.

Topics:

* Clear metadata.
* Strong cultural discovery use case.
* Lower implementation complexity than multimedia categories.
* Path to future expansion.

## 5. User Roles

Define:

* Curators / discoverers.
* Supporters.
* Readers.
* Governance participants.
* Beacon team.
* Future publishers or sponsors.

## 6. Recommendation Lifecycle

Explain:

* Book page creation.
* Series-level canonical pages for recognized book series during MVP.
* Curator stake lock.
* Support transactions.
* Milestone progress.
* Badge minting and upgrades.
* Reward eligibility.

## 7. Reward Model

Document draft milestone reward mechanics.

Include:

* Curator reward.
* Early supporter reward.
* Treasury allocation.
* Badge reputation layer.
* Known limitations and parameters requiring simulation.

## 8. NFT Badge and Reputation System

Explain badges as proof of taste and participation, not ownership of book IP.

Topics:

* Supporter number.
* Timestamp.
* Badge tier evolution.
* Profile reputation.
* Category-level curator status.

## 9. Treasury Model

Explain:

* Community Treasury.
* Operating Reserve.
* Draft 80% / 20% treasury inflow split.
* Public dashboard.
* Spending categories.
* Possible phase-down policy.

## 10. Staking Model

Explain:

* Treasury SOL staking.
* Locked recommender stake staking.
* Conservative native staking preference.
* Yield allocation options.
* Liquidity and risk controls.

## 11. Governance

Explain:

* Participation-based voting eligibility.
* No governance token at launch.
* Constitutional, treasury, and operational decision categories.
* Community approval requirement for new revenue sources.
* Proposal thresholds and quorum assumptions.

## 12. Revenue Model

Explain acceptable revenue sources and approval process.

Potential sources:

* Treasury share from activity.
* Staking yield.
* Affiliate revenue.
* Clearly labeled sponsored placements.
* Premium analytics for publishers and power users.

State that new revenue streams require community approval.

## 13. Risk Analysis

Cover:

* Self-farming.
* Ponzi perception.
* Popularity bias.
* Stalled recommendations.
* Governance fatigue.
* Treasury management risk.
* Staking risk.
* Legal and regulatory risk.
* Content and IP risk.

## 14. Technical Architecture

Explain high-level system boundaries:

* Web app.
* Backend API.
* Solana contracts.
* Treasury dashboard.
* Indexing and analytics.

## 15. Roadmap

Suggested phases:

* Phase 0: Specification, simulation, legal review.
* Phase 1: Books MVP on devnet/localnet.
* Phase 2: Mainnet beta with limited parameters.
* Phase 3: Governance and treasury programs.
* Phase 4: Additional content categories.

## 16. Open Questions

List unresolved decisions before launch:

* Final reward formula.
* Badge minting architecture.
* Governance system implementation.
* Treasury custody and multisig design.
* Legal jurisdiction and compliance requirements.
* Book metadata licensing.
* Duplicate-risk detection and manual review operations.
