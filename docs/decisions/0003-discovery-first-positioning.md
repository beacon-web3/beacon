# 0003: Discovery-First Positioning

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-21

## Context

Beacon includes financial commitment, milestone rewards, treasury flows, and
staking concepts. These mechanics could be misread as a passive yield product or
as a guaranteed-profit system if the project is framed poorly.

The product thesis is about rewarding taste, early discovery, and public
curation reputation.

## Decision

Beacon must be positioned as a discovery and reputation network, not as a yield
product, investment product, or guaranteed-return mechanism.

Financial rewards reinforce discovery behavior but should not be the primary
reason to use the platform.

## Alternatives Considered

### Yield-first positioning

- Pros: Easier to explain to some crypto-native users.
- Cons: Creates legal, trust, and product risks; attracts mercenary behavior;
  weakens the cultural discovery thesis.
- Rejected.

### Pure social discovery positioning

- Pros: Lower financial-risk perception.
- Cons: Fails to explain why SOL-backed support and milestone rewards exist.
- Rejected as incomplete.

## Consequences

- Product copy must avoid guaranteed-profit, passive-income, or investment-return
  language.
- Support payments should be described as conviction, not refundable votes.
- Staking yield should be framed as treasury management, not user-facing yield.
