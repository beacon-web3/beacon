# 0004: No Governance Token at Launch

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-21

## Context

Beacon needs governance over treasury use, future revenue sources, and major
protocol rules. A governance token could add complexity, legal risk, speculation,
and incentives that conflict with participation-based discovery reputation.

## Decision

Beacon will not launch with a governance token.

Initial governance should be based on Beacon participation, such as creating a
recommendation, supporting a recommendation, or holding a participation badge.

## Alternatives Considered

### Launch a governance token immediately

- Pros: Familiar crypto governance pattern and easy voting-weight mechanism.
- Cons: Speculation risk, legal uncertainty, incentive distortion, and premature
  complexity.
- Rejected for launch.

### No governance participation at all

- Pros: Simpler launch.
- Cons: Weakens community legitimacy for treasury and revenue decisions.
- Rejected as a long-term model.

## Consequences

- Governance implementation should not assume a token-based voting model.
- Revenue model changes should require community approval through documented
  participation-based rules.
- Future governance token discussion requires a new decision record.
