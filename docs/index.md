# Beacon Knowledge Bundle

Beacon is a books-first decentralized discovery marketplace on Solana.
This bundle captures product, architecture, tokenomics, and API knowledge
as OKF-conformant markdown documents.

## Product

* [Vision](product/vision.md) - Long-term thesis, positioning, and design principles.
* [MVP](product/mvp.md) - Books-first MVP scope, exclusions, and acceptance criteria.
* [User Stories](product/user-stories.md) - User-facing behavior and acceptance criteria for all roles.
* [Governance](product/governance.md) - Draft governance model, voting, and decision categories.
* [Treasury](product/treasury.md) - Treasury structure, custody phases, and funding model.
* [Risks](product/risks.md) - Known product, economic, security, and trust risks.
* [Roadmap](product/roadmap.md) - Phase-based product and launch roadmap.
* [Assumptions](product/assumptions.md) - Draft assumptions tracked with review status.
* [Open Questions](product/open-questions.md) - Unresolved product, economic, and governance questions.
* [White Paper Outline](product/whitepaper-outline.md) - Working outline for a future white paper.
